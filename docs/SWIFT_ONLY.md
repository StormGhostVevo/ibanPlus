# SWIFT-only countries (not supported)

These countries are not in Airwallex's "Local payout coverage" table, or are marked ** there and left out on purpose. The only way to pay them is an international SWIFT transfer, so the library does not support them: `getConfiguredCountries()` leaves them out and `validateBankDetails` rejects them.

Count: 125 of the 205 countries in the payout requirements doc. Countries marked "Listed as local, excluded" are in Airwallex's table with a ** and were excluded by choice.
